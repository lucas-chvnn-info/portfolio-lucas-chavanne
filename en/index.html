Diploma project (TPI)

Domo

A fully local, open-source home automation system. Sensors on Pico 2W and Pi Zero 2W publish their measurements over MQTT to a Raspberry Pi 5 that stores, displays and reacts, with no cloud service at all.

  • Light, sound, water-leak and motion sensors, with camera capture on detection
  • Shelly plugs controlled and metered from the same interface
  • Remote firmware updates for the Picos (OTA)
  • Phone alerts via ntfy
  • GitLab CI pipeline for testing and deployment
  • Raspberry Pi 5
  • Pico 2W
  • MicroPython
  • Mosquitto
  • Telegraf
  • InfluxDB
  • Grafana
  • Flask
  • GitLab CI

The journey of a measurement

  1. SensorThe Pico 2W reads the sensor and publishes to an MQTT topic.
  2. BrokerMosquitto on the Pi 5 receives and redistributes.
  3. StorageTelegraf writes the measurement into InfluxDB.
  4. DisplayGrafana plots the history in real time.
  5. ReactionThe Flask API triggers the action or the ntfy alert.
What the hardware taught me
  • The Pi's 3V3 pin tops out around 300 mA: beyond that, an external regulator is needed.
  • The Pico's Wi-Fi transmission spikes need a 470 µF and a 100 nF capacitor to stay stable.
  • A simple NPN transistor and a resistor are enough to drive a fan, no relay needed.
  • The Camera Module 3 is not detected on its own: you have to force the imx708 overlay.
